## Detailed Citations & Valor Chronicles

### 1. Major Sandeep Unnikrishnan, AC
- **Decoration:** **Ashoka Chakra (Posthumous)**
- **Unit:** 51 Special Action Group (NSG) / 7 Bihar
- **Operation:** Operation Black Tornado (26/11 Mumbai, 2008)
- **Date of Supreme Sacrifice:** 28 November 2008
- **Iconic Battle Quote:** "Do not come up, I will handle them."
- **Valor Chronicle:** During the 26/11 Mumbai terrorist attacks at the Taj Mahal Palace Hotel, Major Sandeep Unnikrishnan led the elite NSG 51 SAG commando team to rescue hostages from the terror siege. When his fellow commando was hit by enemy fire, he engaged the terrorists alone in fierce close combat, drawing fire away from his team before making the supreme sacrifice.
- **Portrait Image URL:** [sandeep-unnikrishnan.jpg](https://dhurandar-assets.pages.dev/heroes/sandeep-unnikrishnan.jpg)
- **Official Citations:**
  - Ashoka Chakra — India's highest peacetime military decoration for conspicuous bravery
  - NSG Special Action Group Commander
  - Operation Black Tornado Hero
- **External Memorial Links:**
  - [National War Memorial Entry](https://nationalwarmemorial.gov.in)
  - [MHA Gallantry Citation](https://www.gallantryawards.gov.in)

### 2. Captain Vikram Batra, PVC
- **Decoration:** **Param Vir Chakra (Posthumous)**
- **Unit:** 13 Jammu and Kashmir Rifles
- **Operation:** Operation Vijay (Kargil War, 1999)
- **Date of Supreme Sacrifice:** 7 July 1999
- **Iconic Battle Quote:** "Yeh Dil Maange More!"
- **Valor Chronicle:** Leading from the front at 17,000 feet in the Kargil heights, Captain Batra recaptured Point 5140 in fierce hand-to-hand combat. Later, during the assault on Point 4875, he fearlessly exposed himself to heavy enemy machine gun fire to rescue an injured junior officer, laying down his life with unmatched valor.
- **Portrait Image URL:** [vikram-batra.jpg](https://dhurandar-assets.pages.dev/heroes/vikram-batra.jpg)
- **Official Citations:**
  - Param Vir Chakra — India's highest military decoration for most conspicuous bravery
  - Sher Shah of Kargil
  - Captor of Point 5140 & Point 4875
- **External Memorial Links:**
  - [Param Vir Chakra Citation](https://www.gallantryawards.gov.in)
  - [National War Memorial Entry](https://nationalwarmemorial.gov.in)

### 6. Major Somnath Sharma, PVC
- **Decoration:** **Param Vir Chakra (Posthumous)**
- **Unit:** 4 Kumaon Regiment
- **Operation:** Battle of Badgam (1947 Indo-Pak War)
- **Date of Supreme Sacrifice:** 3 November 1947
- **Iconic Battle Quote:** "The enemies are only 50 yards from us. We are heavily outnumbered. I shall not withdraw an inch but fight to our last man and last round."
- **Valor Chronicle:** The first recipient of the Param Vir Chakra, Major Somnath Sharma and his company of 4 Kumaon held the Badgam heights against 500 heavily armed tribal raiders, saving the vital Srinagar airfield and the Kashmir valley despite having his left arm in a plaster cast.

### 9. Flying Officer Nirmal Jit Singh Sekhon, PVC
- **Decoration:** **Param Vir Chakra (Posthumous)**
- **Unit:** No. 18 Squadron (The Flying Bullets), IAF
- **Operation:** 1971 Indo-Pak War (Air Defence of Srinagar Airbase)
- **Date of Supreme Sacrifice:** 14 December 1971
- **Iconic Battle Quote:** "I will engage the Sabres — Srinagar base is clear!"
- **Valor Chronicle:** The only Indian Air Force personnel to be awarded the Param Vir Chakra. Flying Officer Sekhon took off in his Gnat fighter jet while Srinagar runway was being strafed by six PAF F-86 Sabres. He single-handedly engaged the entire enemy formation in low-altitude dogfights, shooting down two Sabres.
